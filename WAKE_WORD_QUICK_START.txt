🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING:

Problem: Wake word button not visible
Solution: Use Chrome or Edge browser, refresh page

Problem: Wake word not detecting
Solution: Click ear icon to enable, speak clearly

Problem: Chatbot doesn't open
Solution: Check microphone permissions in browser

Problem: False triggers
Solution: Click ear icon to disable when not needed
